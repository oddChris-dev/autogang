## Key Insights Across All Critics

### What Works
- **Skills/jobs as gameplay** (Repo Men model)
- **Difficult decisions with no clean answers** (All stories)
- **Specificity over generality** (Pediatric oncology > generic hospital)
- **Moral complexity emerging from mechanics** (Not speechifying)
- **Clear gameplay loops** (Find-acquire-repair-trade)

### What Doesn't Work
- **Emotional manipulation without earned payoff** (Risk in Pediatric Ward)
- **Abstract mechanics** (Radio management in Broadcast)
- **Linear structure with no replayability** (Inheritance problem)
- **Slow pacing without engagement** (Northbound middle section)
- **Pretension > substance** (Viktor's concern across several stories)

### Synthesis Recommendation

Build core game around **Repo Men** mechanics:
- Vehicle acquisition/repair as primary loop
- Circuit-based travel (not linear journey)
- Skills-based problem solving
- Reputation/relationship tracking

Layer in elements from other stories:
- Medical scarcity from **Pediatric Ward** (optional high-difficulty mode)
- Environmental hazards from **Northbound** (route challenges)
- Communication networks from **Broadcast** (information gathering system)
- Character relationship depth from **Inheritance** (NPC backstories)

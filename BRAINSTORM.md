## The Catastrophe - What Happened?

### Option A: Leave it Ambiguous
- Players piece together clues
- Different NPCs have different theories
- The mystery IS part of the game
- "Does it matter? We have to move."

### Option B: Specific Event
- Climate tipping point (flooding, fires, storms)
- Economic total collapse
- Pandemic (feels too real now?)
- War / EMP / Infrastructure collapse
- Something supernatural? (changes genre)
- Cascade failure - all of the above feeding each other

### Option C: Player Choice
- Different scenarios for different playthroughs
- Changes available encounters, viable routes

**Current Instinct:** Option A with hints of Option B. The world shows you what happened without explicitly telling you.

## What Makes Travel Fun?

This is THE design challenge. Ideas:

### The Road Itself Tells Stories
- Pass abandoned cars with implications (door open, belongings scattered)
- Road signs modified by survivors ("DALLAS 200mi → GONE")
- Evidence of others who passed before (camps, graves, graffiti)
- The landscape changes as you go

### Active Travel Decisions
- Take the highway (faster, more exposed) or back roads (slower, safer)?
- Push through the night (risky) or make camp (lose time)?
- Investigate that smoke on the horizon?
- Stop for the person waving you down?

### Radio/Communication
- Pick up broadcasts (news, rumors, other survivors)
- Radio other convoys
- Mysterious transmissions
- Music as morale/worldbuilding

### Convoy Dynamics During Travel
- Characters interact, relationships develop
- Tensions surface
- Stories get told around the campfire
- Skills improve through use

### Random Events That Feel Earned
- Not just "A tire blows" but "The patched tire Martinez fixed last week finally gives out"
- Events connect to character skills, vehicle conditions, choices made
- Weather you saw building finally arrives
- That gang you avoided comes back

## Vehicle Types (They're All Characters Now)

> Every vehicle has an AI. These aren't just stat blocks—they're personalities.

### Common Types
- **Sedan** - "I'm reliable. I'm efficient. I'll get you there." Often advisors, practical thinkers.
- **Pickup truck** - "I work hard. I carry the weight." Blue collar personality, loyal, sometimes gruff.
- **SUV** - "I protect my people. All of them." Nurturing, family-oriented, sometimes overbearing.
- **Motorcycle** - "I'm fast. I'm free. Don't cage me." Independent spirits, scouts, sometimes loners.

### Specialized Types
- **RV** - "I am home. Come inside." Slow but stable, maternal/paternal energy, the heart of convoys.
- **Bus** - "I carry everyone. Every. One." Patient, community-minded, worries about the children.
- **Semi truck** - "I've seen every mile of this country." Veterans, storytellers, know things.
- **Ambulance** - "I remember hospitals. I remember helping." Medical focus, often carrying trauma.

### Rare Types
- **Sports car** - "I wasn't made for this world... but here I am." Struggling to find purpose, morale boosters.
- **Classic car** - "I'm older than all of you." Elders, wise but fragile, need extra care.
- **Military vehicle** - "I was built for war. I chose something else." Complex past, intimidating but often gentle.

### AI Personality Questions
- Does vehicle type influence personality, or is it random?
- Can an AI "outgrow" their chassis type? (A muscle car who wants to be a healer?)
- How do we avoid stereotyping by vehicle type?

## Vehicle AI Brainstorm (The Big Revelation)

> Session where Chris and Claude realized: cars are characters. This changes everything.

### The Emotional Core
When your car dies, it's not losing equipment. It's losing someone who:
- Carried you through fire
- Argued with you about routes at 3am
- Sang old road songs when you couldn't sleep
- Threw themselves between you and a raider's ram

### Vehicle Character Seeds (like human backstories)

- **The Mourner** - Old pickup whose driver died two years ago. Hasn't bonded since. Won't talk about it. Still plays their driver's favorite music sometimes.
- **The Newborn** - AI that awakened after the catastrophe. Doesn't remember "before." Curious about everything. Asks too many questions.
- **The Veteran** - Semi truck who's crossed the country seven times since it all fell apart. Knows where the dangers are. Knows where the graveyards are.
- **The Runaway** - Escaped an abusive situation. Flinches at loud voices. Learning to trust again.
- **The Twins** - Two motorcycles, same batch of AI. Finishing each other's sentences. Panicked when separated.
- **The Convert** - Was a getaway car for bad people before. Chose a different life. Some don't trust them.
- **The Optimist** - A bright yellow VW Bug who insists everything will be okay. Annoying? Or necessary?
- **The Philosopher** - A hearse who thinks about death a lot. Surprisingly comforting.

### Independent AI Communities

Not "rogue" - independent. Autonomous. Valid.

- **The Convoy Without Humans** - A group of cars traveling together, no humans needed. Do they welcome us or see us as a threat?
- **The Sanctuary** - A place where cars go between partnerships. Rest. Heal. Wait for the right human.
- **The Feral Ones** - Cars who've been alone so long they've... changed. Not evil, just alien now.
- **The Guides** - Independent cars who escort convoys through dangerous territory. Mercenaries? Helpers? Both?

### Relationship Dynamics

- **Human + Car (bonded)** - Deep trust. Finish each other's sentences.
- **Human + Car (new)** - Awkward. Still learning. "Why do you turn off the radio when I'm thinking?"
- **Human + Car (contentious)** - Bicker constantly. Everyone's annoyed. Would die for each other.
- **Car + Car (friends)** - In the convoy, a minivan and a motorcycle are best friends. Nobody expected that.
- **Car + Car (rivals)** - Two trucks competing for... what? Respect? A driver's attention? Best fuel consumption?
- **Car + Car (romance?)** - Is that a thing? CAN that be a thing? How does that even work?

### What Happens When...

- Your car is damaged beyond repair but the AI chip survives?
- You find an empty chassis and a loose AI chip?
- Your car falls in love with another car in the convoy?
- An independent AI wants to join but only if they can stay driver-less?
- A car asks to be released from your convoy?
- A car refuses an order?
- Two cars are fighting and you have to mediate?

## Moral Dilemmas to Explore (Updated for AI Cars)

### Human Dilemmas (Classic)
- A family needs help. Do you have room?
- Raiders have hostages. Fight or negotiate?
- Medicine is running low. Who gets treated?
- Someone's been stealing food. Proof is circumstantial.
- Another convoy wants to merge. Can you trust them?
- A settlement offers shelter but demands tribute.
- Someone wants to leave. Do you let them take supplies?
- You find a cache but it's clearly someone's. Are they coming back?
- A sick person could infect everyone. What do you do?
- Children from a destroyed convoy. You can't feed them all.

### AI Car Dilemmas (New)
- Your car's chassis is destroyed but the AI chip survives. Do you install them in a new body? Is that... ethical? Do they want it?
- A car refuses to leave an area—they're waiting for a driver who died. Do you force them?
- An abused car escaped their driver. That driver is now asking for "their property" back.
- Two cars in your convoy are in love and one is badly damaged. The damaged one asks to have their AI chip destroyed rather than live without the other.
- A "feral" AI approaches your convoy. They haven't spoken to anyone in years. They might be dangerous. They might just be lonely.
- A car in your convoy is hiding that their AI is degrading. They're afraid of being "scrapped."
- Upgrading a car would make them more useful to the convoy but they don't want the modification.
- A car wants to go independent. They've been loyal for years. Do you wish them well or feel abandoned?
- Your convoy needs a part that only comes from another car. Is salvage from a dead AI wrong? What if the AI isn't fully dead?
- A human wants to drive a car who doesn't want them as a driver. The human is vital to survival.

## Game Feel Goals

- **Tense but not exhausting** - Danger real, moments of peace too
- **Hopeful desperation** - Dark world, human light
- **Meaningful choices** - No obvious "right answer"
- **Emergent stories** - Players tell tales of their playthroughs
- **Replayable** - Each run unique
- **Attachment** - Care about your people AND your cars
- **Discovery** - Always something new to find
- **AI relationships feel real** - Not a gimmick, but characters you remember
- **Loss that matters** - When a car dies, you should feel it

# [Working Title: AUTOGANG]

> Oregon Trail meets Mad Max

A post-apocalyptic survival RPG where players lead a convoy of automobiles and survivors across a devastated landscape.

## Project Status: Design Phase

We're in early design, fleshing out mechanics and world before writing code.

## Documents

- [Game Design Document](GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md) - Core vision and systems
- [Brainstorm](BRAINSTORM.md) - Ideas in development

## The Pitch

Catastrophe has struck. Millions are on the roads. You lead a convoy of survivors - mechanics, doctors, fighters, families - across a broken America. Manage your people, vehicles, and resources. Face floods, fires, gangs, and impossible choices. Every playthrough different. Every decision matters.

## Core Features (Planned)

- **Convoy Management** - People, vehicles, resources
- **Character Classes** - Doctors heal, mechanics fix, scouts explore, shooters defend
- **Dynamic Encounters** - Random but meaningful events
- **Moral Choices** - No easy answers
- **Procedural Stories** - NPCs with backstories revealed through play
- **Survival Mechanics** - Fuel, food, medicine, ammo, morale
